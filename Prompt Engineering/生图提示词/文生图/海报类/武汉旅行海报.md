# 武汉旅行海报

> 🏠 [[生图提示词]] > [[文生图]] > [[海报类]]
>
> 相关风格：[[广州塔极简海报]] | [[极简中式美学海报]]

```
Create a vertical 2:3 travel poster for WUHAN in a clean flat vector illustration style.

Composition: the upper 75% contains a clear and organized landmark collage, and the lower 25% is warm ivory negative space. At the bottom, place a large thin widely spaced sans-serif title "WUHAN", with a smaller subtitle "武汉" below it.

Focus only on 5 to 7 iconic Wuhan landmarks: Yellow Crane Tower, Wuhan Yangtze River Bridge, East Lake, Wuhan University, Hubei Provincial Museum, Jianghan Customs House, and Guishan TV Tower. Yellow Crane Tower and Wuhan Yangtze River Bridge should be the main visual focus. East Lake and Wuhan University should be secondary focal points. The other landmarks should stay smaller and supportive in the background.

Use a layered pictorial collage layout, not realistic perspective. Foreground may include a small road, a metro train, or a boat. Middle ground should include the river, bridge, park, and the major buildings. Background should include a few skyline buildings and distant towers. Keep the layout balanced, clean, and easy to read, with strong visual hierarchy.

Style: precise thin navy blue outlines, slightly thicker outer contours, clean architectural linework, flat vector illustration, warm ivory background, muted teal water, soft green trees, pale blue glass, muted orange and brick-red accents for historic architecture. Minimal decorative elements. Calm, refined, premium cultural tourism poster aesthetic.

Text control: only the main title "WUHAN", the subtitle "武汉", and at most 3 small Chinese landmark labels such as "黄鹤楼", "东湖", "武汉长江大桥".

Negative prompt: too many small details, cluttered composition, too many labels, too many vehicles, too many birds, hot air balloons, photorealistic, 3D render, watercolor, oil painting, cyberpunk, neon colors, messy background, distorted buildings, random text, blurry image, oversaturated colors.
```
