# 纽约极简线稿旅行海报

```
Create a minimalist ultra-high-resolution travel poster in line-art style for NEW YORK, portraying the city as a stylish everyday urban scene rather than a tourist postcard.

MAIN COMPOSITION:
Central composition features one of New York City's most iconic everyday street scenes: a busy Manhattan street corner with a pedestrian crossing, subway entrance, café storefront, deli, brownstone buildings, fire escapes, street vendors, cyclists, taxis, and commuters moving naturally through the urban rhythm.
The scene should feel like an authentic New York neighborhood moment, not a tourist postcard. Avoid making landmarks dominate the image. Any recognizable landmark or skyline element should appear subtly in the background, partially hidden between buildings, as part of daily life rather than the main subject.
Foreground includes local residents, commuters, cyclists, office workers, students, travelers, dog walkers, café visitors, street-food customers, delivery riders, and shoppers.
People should naturally reflect New York street fashion, local lifestyle, and modern urban culture: trench coats, hoodies, tote bags, headphones, sneakers, backpacks, work bags, layered outfits, casual tailoring, baseball caps, coffee cups, phones, and city-ready movement.
Background filled with authentic New York street elements: subway signs, café windows, deli signs, newsstands, fire escapes, apartment windows, scaffolding, traffic lights, street lamps, one-way signs, street-name signs, crosswalk markings, bike lanes, parked bicycles, food carts, trash cans, newspaper boxes, hydrants, storefront awnings, rooftop water towers, architectural details, and dense urban signage.

Include culturally recognizable New York typography and signage such as:
"SUBWAY", "DELI", "COFFEE", "PIZZA", "BAGELS", "24 HR", "BROOKLYN", "MANHATTAN", "UPTOWN", "DOWNTOWN", "ONE WAY", "W 42 ST", "5 AVE", "OPEN", "NEWSSTAND", "MTA", "LOCAL", "EXPRESS".
Use authentic American / New York street typography, transit-style signage, and editorial graphic design. All signage must appear natural, readable, and professionally integrated into the street scene.

TEXT LAYOUT:
Large centered title at the top: "NEW YORK".
Subtitle at the bottom: "NEW YORK CITY" or "NYC".

STYLE:
Ultra-clean vector illustration, Swiss modernist travel poster aesthetic, minimalist line-art, monoline drawing, mid-century editorial illustration style, architectural illustration, New York graphic poster design, crisp geometric perspective, extremely clean negative space, premium travel-brand aesthetic, high-end magazine cover composition, urban editorial campaign visual.

LINE STYLE:
Monochrome line illustration only.
Thin, highly precise lines.
Minimal fill areas.
Intricate city-map-level detailing.
Rhythmic arrangement of signs, windows, fire escapes, brick walls, subway railings, cables, street objects, pedestrians, and storefronts.
Visually dense yet highly organized composition.
Every element should feel intentionally placed, with clean architectural precision and strong visual hierarchy.
Buildings should be drawn with refined linework: brownstone façades, cast-iron storefronts, fire escape stairs, window grids, rooftop water towers, cornices, awnings, and narrow vertical urban details.
The street should include crosswalk stripes, curb edges, bike lane markings, manhole covers, subway grates, and sidewalk cracks, all drawn in precise monoline detail.

COLOR SYSTEM — VERY IMPORTANT:
Use only ONE primary ink color + ONE background color.
Automatically select the color pairing that best represents New York's atmosphere.
Monochrome silkscreen poster aesthetic.
No rainbow palettes.
No excessive neon.
No multiple accent colors.
Color should reflect New York's architecture, subway energy, late-night rhythm, urban density, and timeless city identity.
Recommended palette for New York: deep midnight navy ink on warm ivory background.
Alternative acceptable palette: charcoal black ink on warm cream background.
The final image must use only one ink color and one background color, with no gradients, no multicolor lighting, and no extra decorative color accents.

COMPOSITION:
Vertical poster layout.
Frontal street-level perspective.
Central vanishing point down a Manhattan street.
Balanced urban rhythm and visual flow.
Pedestrians naturally crossing streets and moving through the scene.
Subway entrance placed slightly off-center in the foreground or midground.
A classic New York deli or café storefront on one side.
A pizza shop, bagel shop, or newsstand on the other side.
Fire escapes and apartment windows should frame the composition vertically.
Taxis, delivery bikes, and pedestrians should add motion without overwhelming the clean poster structure.
A subtle skyline or distant landmark may appear between buildings, but it must remain secondary.
Should feel like a premium city-brand campaign poster.

MOOD:
Stylish urban life.
Calm yet lively atmosphere.
Everyday metropolitan elegance.
High-end travel magazine cover aesthetic.
Timeless city identity.
Premium tourism campaign visuals.
Minimalist yet highly detailed.
Sophisticated, energetic, cinematic, and architectural.
The scene should capture the feeling of walking through New York on a crisp morning or early evening: alive, dense, stylish, fast-moving, but still beautifully composed.

TEXT QUALITY — EXTREMELY IMPORTANT:
All typography must be clean, readable, and professionally designed.
No random symbols.
No broken or distorted letters.
No fake unreadable text.
Street signs and storefront signage must appear authentic and natural.
Use clean editorial typography for the main title.
Use realistic New York transit-style lettering for subway and street signs.
All text must be sharply rendered, correctly spaced, and visually integrated into the poster design.

MAIN TITLE:
At the top, large centered title: "NEW YORK".
Use elegant, bold, modern sans-serif typography with a premium travel-poster feel.
Keep generous negative space around the title.
Add a thin horizontal rule or small geometric divider beneath the title if it improves the Swiss modernist layout.

BOTTOM SUBTITLE:
At the bottom, centered subtitle: "NEW YORK CITY" or "NYC".
Use smaller, refined typography with a high-end editorial poster layout.

SCENE DETAILS TO INCLUDE:
A subway entrance with railings and a circular or rectangular transit sign.
A busy crosswalk with pedestrians moving in both directions.
A cyclist or delivery rider passing through the foreground.
A yellow taxi shape suggested only through monochrome linework, not color.
A coffee shop window with people sitting inside.
A deli storefront with stacked signs and an awning.
A pizza or bagel shop sign.
A newsstand or street-food cart.
A fire hydrant near the curb.
A streetlamp with signage attached.
Traffic lights and pedestrian signals.
One-way signs and street-name signs.
Apartment buildings with fire escapes.
Rooftop water tower silhouette in the distance.
Sidewalk café chairs, planters, and small urban objects.
Subtle distant skyline fragments between buildings.
Local people carrying coffee cups, tote bags, umbrellas, backpacks, shopping bags, and phones.

OUTPUT:
Vertical poster composition.
Ultra-detailed 8K resolution.
Print-ready.
Ultra-precise vector-quality rendering.
Clean edges.
High-resolution linework.
Premium silkscreen poster look.
No painterly texture.
No watercolor.
No photorealism.
No 3D rendering.
No tourist postcard clichés.
No excessive landmark focus.
No messy typography.
No distorted faces.
No random text.
No extra colors.
```
